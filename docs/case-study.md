# Case Study — Bookings & Revenue Intelligence

## Business Problem
Executive finance reporting requires bookings, renewals, revenue, forecast changes, and manual adjustments to reconcile across different source systems and reporting cadences. Static reporting makes it difficult to explain weekly movement and forecast risk.

## Objective
Create a governed analytics platform that combines bookings and revenue data with bottom-up forecasting and variance analysis so finance leaders can understand both current performance and what changed.

## Solution Pattern
1. Consolidate CRM, renewal, revenue, and adjustment data in Snowflake.
2. Standardize customer, contract, stage, and period logic.
3. Build a Power BI semantic model for bookings, revenue, and forecast metrics.
4. Add prior-period / prior-week comparisons.
5. Add bottom-up and live forecast views.
6. Surface variance drivers for executive review.

## Key Capabilities
- bookings by business and region
- renewal activity
- future ACV / contract exposure
- revenue by stage
- bottom-up forecast
- live forecast analysis
- variance analysis
- management adjustments

## Public Portfolio Scope
The public version will recreate the architecture with synthetic customers, bookings, revenue stages, and forecast scenarios.

## Evolution Story
The case study will show how an initial regional reporting model can evolve into a broader enterprise platform with live forecast and variance intelligence.
